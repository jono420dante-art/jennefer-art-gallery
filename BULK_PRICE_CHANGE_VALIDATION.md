# Bulk Price Change Validation

## Protected Browser Review — 21 August 2026

The native Administrator session opened the protected `/admin` Artwork Studio. The batch toolbar now presents **Price Change** beside **Mark Sold**, **Mark Available**, **Move to**, and **Delete**.

After selecting one artwork, the **Price Change** control became active and opened a focused modal. The modal identifies the number of selected artworks, provides independent ZAR and USD update switches, accepts non-negative numeric prices, and states that an enabled blank price removes only that selected currency. It also explicitly states that a price change does not change availability, collection, images, or descriptions.

The modal was closed with **Cancel**. No live artwork or price was changed during browser verification.
